Cube sealed and the player cap — V5

A declared tournament can deal a cube: the organizer picks a community cube, loads their Re:Union decks or pastes a list, and every card of it is given to at most one player (the cube is dealt once, at the first pool, into as many lots of boosters as the tournament has places). That needs a player cap (“Maximum players”), which every other source can now use too: once it is reached nobody else can open a pool, from the site or from a BGA game (409 tournament_full).
