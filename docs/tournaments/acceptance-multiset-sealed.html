Multi-set sealed and random uniques — V3

A declared sealed tournament whose 7 classic single-set boosters are split over several sets (e.g. 3 Skybound Odyssey, 3 Roots of Corruption, 1 Neverending Journey), and a random number of uniques (each booster rolls “1 in N”) available with every source: one set, multi-set, mono-faction. Runs on make dev (API :8080, Vite :5173) + altered-dev-environment, with the lot 1 setup: accounts, bob moderator, carol (see group 0 of docs/tournaments/acceptance-user-tournaments.html).
